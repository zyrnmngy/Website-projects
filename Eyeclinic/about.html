About VisionAR

Pioneering the future of eye care through innovative AR technology and professional excellence

Our Mission

To revolutionize eye care accessibility by combining cutting-edge augmented reality technology with professional optometric standards, ensuring everyone has access to precise, convenient, and affordable eyewear solutions that enhance their vision and quality of life.

Our Story

VisionAR was founded in 2021 by a team of optometrists, technology innovators, and healthcare professionals who recognized the critical gap between traditional eye care services and the growing need for accessible, accurate vision solutions.

Frustrated by the limitations of conventional fitting methods and the barriers many people face in accessing quality eye care, our founders set out to create a technology that could deliver professional-grade results from anywhere in the world.

Today, we're proud to serve over 15,000 customers through our network of 250+ partner clinics, maintaining the highest standards of accuracy and patient satisfaction in the industry.

2021 - 2025

From innovative startup to industry leader, transforming how people access eye care worldwide
